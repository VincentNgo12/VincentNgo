2026 · ESP32-S3 / PIC18 / FreeRTOS

Lower-power sensing firmware

Electrical and Control Engineering Intern
Red Deer Polytechnic · CIM-TAC / Energy Innovation Centre
May–August 2026

Problem
A non-invasive sensing platform needed to use less power while continuing to acquire sensor data.
Decision
I used GPIO interrupts, task notifications, peripheral power gating, and scheduled sleep to reduce the time the hardware spent awake.
Result
Average current draw fell from 85.71 mA to 25.33 mA, a 70.4% reduction.

2025 · STM32 / C / CMSIS-DSP

Sound into light, in real time

Bare-metal audio spectrum visualizer

Problem
Turn live microphone input into responsive LED patterns within the STM32F103’s 20 KB SRAM budget.
Decision
I combined ADC acquisition through DMA, a 1024-point CMSIS-DSP FFT, and timer-controlled PWM so audio capture could continue while the LEDs updated.
Result
FFT processing fell from 28 ms to 15 ms, with 17 ms end-to-end latency. I checked timing with GDB, a logic analyzer, and an oscilloscope.

ESP32 / ESP-IDF / KiCad

NEST wearable health monitor

Sensor acquisition, firmware, and PCB design

Problem
Collect pulse, motion, and environmental readings without display and Wi-Fi tasks starving sensor acquisition.
Decision
I separated acquisition from interface and wireless work across the ESP32’s two cores, using FreeRTOS synchronization and SPI DMA to coordinate the tasks.
Result
I designed and assembled a mixed-signal PCB, checked power rails and communication buses, and integrated the sensors into a 3D-printed wearable prototype.
