01 / A little background

A curious mind.
A hands-on approach.

I like working where software meets the physical world.

That has taken me from bare-metal signal processing to connected sensors, mixed-signal circuit boards, and flight software. I enjoy following an idea all the way through: understanding the problem, building a prototype, and finding out what happens on real hardware.

At Red Deer Polytechnic’s CIM-TAC / Energy Innovation Centre, I worked on low-power sensing, firmware updates, PCB design, and closed-loop control. My work with AlbertaSat’s Ex-Alta 3 CubeSat also brought me into embedded Linux, Rust, and hardware integration.

01 — SIGNAL INTO LIGHT

Embedded C · STM32 · Signal processing

Sound, made visible.

A bare-metal audio spectrum visualizer that transforms microphone input into responsive LED patterns, using a 1024-point FFT, DMA, and timer-driven PWM.

17 ms end-to-end latency · 20 KB SRAM budget

Small form. Many senses.
